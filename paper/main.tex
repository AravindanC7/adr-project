\section*{Ethics Statement}
% TODO (Harshini): check the wording and fill [N] once the pilot ends (Fri Nov 13).
Our human pilot was a small class pilot with [N] classmates aged 18 or older (target: about ten).
Prof.~Vivek Gupta approved a small classmate pilot for this course. The pilot was \emph{not}
reviewed or approved by an Institutional Review Board, so the data is used only for this class
project. Participants gave consent before starting. We used partial disclosure: before the
session we described in general terms what we record and why, and after the last question a
full debrief explained the hidden checks and let participants remove their answers. We did not
collect names, emails or student IDs; responses are stored under a random session code, and
telemetry records only timings and counts, never the keys typed. Only aggregate totals are
reported, and the row-level pilot data is deleted when the course ends in December 2026. The detection system never blocks a respondent automatically; it only pauses a session
for human review, because even a 1\% false-positive rate would flag honest people.
